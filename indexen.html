Our Solutions

Our Purpose

Transforming financial challenges into opportunities for sustainable growth.

Over the years, Prime Partners has played an active role in supporting the growth of Mozambican companies, facilitating access to strategic financing for investment projects and operations. Our team has already supported companies in mobilizing more than 300 million Meticals in financing for CAPEX and OPEX, connecting businesses to Banks and strategic investors.

As part of our long-term commitment to the development of the business sector in Mozambique, we have set a goal to support Mozambican companies in obtaining at least 3 Billion Meticals in financing by 2030, contributing to sustainable growth, innovation, and the strengthening of the private sector.
